## Surgical Planning for Cranial Implant Planning

1. [op=user_choice] In the "Spine CT" section, select the CT volume for processing.
2. [op=extension_op] Click the "Next" button in the "1. Load Image Volume" page.
3. [op=user_interaction] Manually adjust the boundaries of the ROI.
4. [op=user_choice] Choose the "Instrumented Levels:". Choose the "# Sides:". Choose the "Approach Direction:".
5. [op=extension_op] Click the "Next" button in the "2. Define Surgical Region of Interest (ROI)" page.
6. [op=slicer_op] In the toolbar, turn on "slice intersection visibility". In the slice intersection interaction options, turn on "set interaction", then enable both "Translate" and "Rotate".
7. [op=user_interaction] Manually adjust the rotation angle of the red slice.
8. [op=extension_op] Click the "Place a control point" button to activate the point placement.
9. [op=user_interaction]Manually click in the 2D views to add fiducial points for one part of the spine.
10. [op=extension_op] Click the "Place a control point" button to inactivate the point placement.
11. [op=branch_op] If all points are configured for all parts, jump to step 12. If not, jump to step 7.
12. [op=slicer_op] In the slice intersection interaction options of the toolbar, turn off "set interaction".
13. [op=user_choice] Set the Level/Side/Landmarks following the original selection widget.
14. [op=extension_op] Click the "Next" button in the "3. Place the Landmarks" page.
15. [op=user_choice]Choose the "Choose the puncture site". Choose the "Select screw diametermm".
16. [op=user_interaction] Manually adjust the start and end position of puncture site in 2D views.
17. [op=extension_op] Click the "Update" button.
18. [op=extension_op] Click the "OK" button.
19. [op=branch_op] If every puncture site is configured, jump to step 20. If not, jump to step 15.
20. [op=extension_op] Click the "Next" button in the "4. Adjust Screws" page.
21. [op=extension_op] Click the "Grade Screws" button.
22. [op=review_op] Review the generated Grade Screws output Table following the original results table.
23. [op=extension_op] Click the "Next" button in the "Grading Step" page.
